WordKeeper — Chrome Extension
==============================

Save words while browsing. Double-click or right-click any word to keep it.

Installing
----------
1. Go to `chrome://extensions`
2. Enable "Developer mode" (top right)
3. Click "Load unpacked" and select this folder

Usage
-----
- **Double-click** a word on any page to save it
- **Right-click** a selected word → "Save to WordKeeper"
- Click the extension icon to view your saved words
- **Copy All** — copies your word list to clipboard
- **Email to Myself** — opens your email client with the list pre-filled
- **Clear All** — removes all saved words
- Click the × next to a word to delete just that one

Words sync across all Chrome browsers where you're signed in.
